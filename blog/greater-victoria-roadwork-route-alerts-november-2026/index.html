Greater Victoria Roadwork and Route Alerts for Nov. 2–6, 2026

Current Saanich, North Saanich and Highway 1 traffic impacts for Greater Victoria lessons, commutes and road-test planning.

By Shanaya's Driving School · Published November 1, 2026 · 5 min read · Greater Victoria Road Safety

If you have a driving lesson, school commute or ICBC road test in Greater Victoria this week, check the official traffic notices before leaving and allow extra time. Current notices affect Saanich, North Saanich and overnight travel on Highway 1. This is a dated summary from Shanaya's Driving School; the municipal and provincial notices remain the controlling sources.

Current closures and lane restrictions

Saanich

  • Millgrove Street: A water-main upgrade is scheduled for Monday, November 2, from 8 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. The road is closed to through traffic, with local and emergency access maintained.
  • McKenzie Avenue at Gordon Head Road and Shelbourne Street: Single-lane traffic is scheduled for Tuesday, November 3, from 8:30 a.m. to 3 p.m.
  • Piedmont Drive: A water-main project continues through November 30, with a road closure between Del Monte Avenue and Santa Clara Avenue during the listed work hours.

The District of Saanich traffic advisoriessay signs and traffic-control personnel will guide drivers. Follow temporary signs rather than relying on a familiar route.

North Saanich

Dean Park Road will be closed to through traffic during working hours from November 3 to 6 for the Capital Regional District's Bear Hill Trunk Main Extension Project. A temporary detour will be in place. Local access will be maintained, and East Saanich Road may have alternating single-lane traffic. Read theDistrict of North Saanich noticebefore travelling.

Overnight Highway 1 work

DriveBC lists overnight blasting between the View Royal and Saanich/McKenzie Avenue areas from October 6 to November 30. The notice describes lane closures in both directions, intermittent stoppages of up to 10 minutes during blasts, weekday work from 8 p.m. to 6 a.m. and Saturday work from 8 p.m. to 8 a.m.

A separate DriveBC notice for the Bus on Shoulder project covers the Saanich/McKenzie Avenue and View Royal/ Colwood Bridge corridor. It lists weekday night work from 8 p.m. to 5 a.m., periodic five-minute stoppages and a 50 km/h speed restriction. This is a separate event from the blasting notice. Check thelive DriveBC feed before travelling because construction details can change.

A route checklist for lessons and road tests

  1. Check the Saanich advisories, the North Saanich noticeand DriveBC before leaving.
  2. Add a time buffer for a detour, queue or traffic-control stop.
  3. Follow the posted temporary speed, lane and turn instructions.
  4. Contact the school or testing office early if your appointment is time-sensitive.
  5. Use route changes to practise transferable skills rather than trying to memorise a test route.

For road-test preparation, practise observation, speed control, lane positioning, shoulder checks and safe decisions around changing traffic. ICBC's Road Test Skills Explainerdescribes skills an examiner may assess; it does not publish a route to memorise.

Why gradual route practice matters

One saved review from Vanessa Nicdao describes her son starting on narrow residential streets and later driving confidently on the highway after lessons. That is one family's account, not a typical timeline or a promise of readiness, but it illustrates why practice should expand by skill and traffic demand.
