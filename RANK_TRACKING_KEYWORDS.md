## Setup notes

1. **Google Search Console is your ground truth** — it shows real impressions and average position for terms you already rank for, free. Start there before paying for a tracker; you'll likely find terms in this list you're already on page 2–3 for, which are the cheapest wins.
2. Track **mobile separately** — most driving-school searches are mobile, and local-pack positioning differs.
3. Set a **baseline snapshot now**, before the October 19 GLP change drives a search surge, so you can prove movement.
4. Realistic expectations: Tier 1/2/5 local terms are winnable in 3–6 months. Tier 3 knowledge-test terms compete with ICBC's own site and national practice-test networks — 6–12 months minimum.
