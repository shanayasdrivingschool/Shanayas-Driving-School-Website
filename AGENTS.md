# Content work

For planning, writing, editing, or reviewing public SEO articles, FAQs, service-page copy, author information, or related content metadata, read and follow [SEO content rules](SEO%20Blogs%20Data/CONTENT_RULES.md).

These rules are project editorial requirements derived from the supplied Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines. They do not impose a workflow on unrelated application development.

Treat the research CSVs as planning inputs, not verified publication sources. Preserve them unless the user requests edits. Complete drafts and independent work when evidence is missing; identify unresolved claims without inventing facts or claiming a review occurred.
