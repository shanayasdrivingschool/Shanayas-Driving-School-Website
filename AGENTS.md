# Content work

For researching, planning, writing, editing, or reviewing public SEO articles, FAQs, service-page copy, author information, or related content metadata, read and follow [SEO content rules](SEO%20Blogs%20Data/CONTENT_RULES.md). This includes public copy embedded in application files and generated page metadata.

These rules are project editorial requirements derived from the supplied Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines. They do not impose a workflow on unrelated application development.

Treat the research CSVs as planning inputs, not verified publication sources. Preserve them unless the user requests edits. Complete drafts and independent work when evidence is missing; identify unresolved claims without inventing facts or claiming a review occurred.

## Before drafting or editing

- Use `CONTENT_RULES.md` as the full editorial reference; the checklist here summarizes its requirements. Explicit user instructions determine task scope, while factual claims still need appropriate evidence.
- Inspect the current article inventory and relevant draft notes. Treat the article status audit as a dated snapshot. Update existing coverage when it serves the same reader need.
- Read [company knowledge](COMPANY_KNOWLEDGE.md) and follow references to the relevant business records. Existing website copy and company summaries do not independently verify disputed or current claims.
- For new content, record the short brief from section 10: reader, question, location, outcome, scope, overlap, evidence gaps, original contribution, responsible author or organization, required review, next step, and maintenance dates. For a focused edit, check the affected claims and nearby context proportionately.

## Evidence and attribution

- Inspect current authoritative sources for licensing, eligibility, test policies, safety, and insurance claims. Distinguish legal requirements, official guidance, instructor recommendations, and examples; retain relevant conditions and effective dates.
- Verify school prices, promotions, services, pickup arrangements, and credentials against applicable business records or dated owner instructions. Record and resolve conflicts rather than silently choosing a favourable claim.
- Keep a claim log in working notes with the inspected source, supporting evidence, actual check date, applicable effective date, and unresolved gaps. Link public claims to specific supporting public pages; keep private records private.
- Never invent statistics, quotations, student stories, teaching experience, credentials, review dates, or pass guarantees. Directory inclusion does not establish ICBC approval, recommendation, or endorsement.
- When using student feedback, read [saved reviews](SEO%20Blogs%20Data/reviews.md) and follow section 7.1. Preserve the raw file, attribution, exact quotation text, material qualifications, and individual-outcome limits. Separate customer text from owner replies and interface labels; a saved snapshot does not verify current ratings, offers, or typical results.
- Obtain appropriate subject review for practical driving instruction and other claims requiring it under section 2. Record a reviewer only when the review actually occurred. Continue independent drafting while evidence or review is pending.

## Public copy and metadata

- Answer the reader's central question early, use Canadian spelling, and make the content useful for Victoria, British Columbia. Add a useful explanation, comparison, checklist, verified local detail, or documented instructor insight.
- Avoid filler, keyword stuffing, arbitrary word counts or FAQ quotas, near-duplicate location pages, unsupported superlatives, and ranking promises.
- State verified price duration, currency, inclusions, charges, tax treatment, and offer conditions where relevant. Label budget calculations as examples with clear units and assumptions; do not imply a typical lesson count or guaranteed readiness.
- Keep titles, descriptions, social previews, captions, structured data, and visible copy consistent. Use actual publication, update, and review dates; keep internal notes and verification placeholders out of public output.
- Use relevant internal links and confirmed booking destinations. Keep booking prompts helpful and the main answer accessible.

## Verification and handoff

- Apply the final checklist in section 10 before calling a complete page ready. Check mobile rendering and relevant links, and keep maintained Markdown drafts consistent with implemented copy.
- Report only checks actually performed. Distinguish historical verification from current checks; a build does not establish deployment, factual accuracy, or subject review.
- Record page quality and intent satisfaction separately. Use **Ready**, **Needs revision**, or **Draft awaiting evidence/review**, with specific reasons and remaining checks. For a limited edit, describe its verification without implying a full-page review.
- Record publication state separately as **draft only**, **implemented locally**, **live confirmed**, or **publication unverified**, with the inspected file or URL and actual inspection date. Readiness does not itself publish content or establish indexing or ranking.
- Missing evidence or subject review limits readiness; it does not create an additional permission requirement for ordinary edits. Finish independent work and ask only for material information that available sources cannot resolve.
