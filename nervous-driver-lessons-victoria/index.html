Support for nervous beginners and returning drivers

Shanaya's Driving School offers lessons for nervous beginners and drivers returning after a break. Tell us about your experience, concerns and goals before booking so we can discuss a suitable starting point in Greater Victoria.

You may want help with a particular situation, such as parking, lane changes or a future road test. You can ask for a clear explanation, repetition or a discussion about breaks. Your instructor considers your skills and the conditions as well as your comfort when planning practice.

There is no promised timetable for confidence or test readiness. The purpose of these lessons is driver education: learning and practising driving skills with support, without pressure to match another learner's progress.

What would make starting easier?

Before booking, tell us which situations concern you and how you prefer instructions to be explained. You do not need to share a detailed personal history to describe the support you want.

  • A situation you would like help with
  • Any communication or accessibility needs to discuss

Who these lessons are for

You can enquire as an adult beginner, a returning driver or someone who has lost confidence after a difficult driving experience. We will discuss whether the available lesson format suits your needs.

Progress at an individual pace

One learner may need more repetition than another. Discuss progress through specific skills, what remains difficult and the next practice goal, rather than setting a fixed number of lessons.

How nervous-driver lessons work

  1. Meet and discuss the lesson

    Talk through your goals, the vehicle and how to communicate concerns or request a break.

  2. Begin at a suitable level

    Your instructor assesses your starting skills and chooses a setting suited to the lesson and current conditions.

  3. Focus on one concern

    Agree on a specific skill to practise, with time for explanations and repetition where appropriate.

  4. Connect practice to your goals

    Discuss the journeys you hope to make and the skills still needed. Test preparation can be considered separately when relevant.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enquire if I feel very nervous about driving?

Yes. Tell us about your concerns before booking. We can discuss the available lesson format, pacing and starting point so you can decide whether it suits you.

Will I have to drive on busy roads straight away?

There is no fixed first-lesson route. Discuss your concerns before starting; your instructor will assess your skills and choose an appropriate setting and progression.

What if I need a break during a lesson?

Discuss how to communicate this before the lesson. Tell your instructor if you feel unable to continue so they can help arrange a safe stop and discuss the next step. See our Student Safety Policy for the school's approach to concerns.

What happens in the first lesson?

Begin by discussing your experience, goals and concerns. Ask the instructor to explain the vehicle and proposed lesson focus, then review what you worked on and what needs further practice.

How many lessons will I need?

There is no fixed number. Your starting skills, practice opportunities and goals all matter. Discuss progress with the instructor before committing to further lessons; feeling more confident does not by itself establish test readiness.

Is it okay to start learning as an adult?

Yes. Tell us you are an adult beginner so we can discuss an appropriate first lesson. You do not need previous driving experience to enquire.

Can I prepare for the road test?

Ask about a Road Test Prep lesson or Mock Test Evaluation when booking. These are separate course options; a mock test is a practice assessment, not an ICBC test or a guarantee of passing.
