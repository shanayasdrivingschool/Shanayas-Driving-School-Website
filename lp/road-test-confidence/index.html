Frequently Asked Questions

Do I receive all 30 hours from the start?

No. Both packages include 15 initial hours: ten lessons of 90 minutes. After an unsuccessful qualifying first test, five more lessons are included; after a second, three more; after a third, two more. That is up to 15 conditional extra hours and 30 hours in total. Each block is available for the next covered attempt, subject to programme conditions.

What happens if I pass on my first attempt?

Your conditional retest support ends when you pass. The initial ten lessons are included regardless of the result. Outstanding payments and any refund rights are governed by your written purchase and refund terms; passing does not itself create a refund promise.

Does this guarantee I will pass?

No. The package provides a defined amount of training and conditional retest support, not unlimited lessons or a guaranteed pass. ICBC determines your road-test result.

Are the road-test car and ICBC fees included?

Both packages include the training vehicle during lessons. The $1,999 CAD + GST package excludes test-day vehicle rental. The $2,599 CAD + GST Plus package includes the school car for the first covered test and up to three retries following unsuccessful tests, subject to booking and coverage rules. All ICBC testing fees and applicable government charges are separate. Book the official road test directly with ICBC and coordinate school-car availability with our team.

Can a beginner join, and where are lessons available?

The programme may suit different experience levels. Discuss your experience and training needs with the team before choosing a package; 15 initial hours may not meet every learner’s needs. Lessons are offered in Greater Victoria, including Victoria and Langford. Confirm eligible licence classes, pickup location, test centre, coverage dates and scheduling before you enrol.

What if I need extra lessons or more than four attempts?

The package has no additional included coaching blocks after the fourth covered attempt. Further lessons or road-test vehicle services can be arranged separately at the applicable rate, subject to availability. You can also ask about extra training before an earlier attempt.

Can I pay in instalments?

Eligible instalment options, including Klarna where available, may apply to your purchase. Ask the team which options are available for your package. Purchase limits, approval, interest or fees and repayment schedules are set by the provider. Approval is not guaranteed.

What should I confirm before paying?

Ask the team to explain which tests qualify, the package coverage period, lesson-completion requirements, available test-day vehicle dates, and cancellation and refund terms. Review your written package terms before purchase. Requesting a callback does not book a test or commit you to buying a package.
