Why learn with Shanaya's Driving School in Langford?

  • Prepare for Westshore journeys. Work towards the driving you need around Goldstream Avenue, Millstream Village and Westhills, with lessons focused on your current skills and everyday destinations.
  • Build highway and roundabout confidence. Discuss lane changes, merging and roundabouts as lesson goals. Your instructor chooses suitable practice locations and introduces more demanding traffic as your skills develop.
  • Get focused ICBC test preparation. Prepare for a Class 7 road test or a Class 5 road test where required, with feedback on observation, vehicle control, parking and decision-making. We teach transferable skills rather than promise an examiner's route.
  • Make lessons easier to fit into your day. Learn in an automatic dual-control vehicle and arrange door-to-door pickup. Both beginners and returning drivers can discuss a lesson plan with our Westshore team.

Questions about Langford and Westshore lessons

Where is your Langford branch, and is it separate from James Bay?

Our Langford Campus (Westshore) is at Unit 124, 2770 Leigh Rd, Langford, BC V9B 4G1, Canada. It is a separate physical branch from our Victoria Campus in James Bay. Both use 250-542-3673 and book@drivingschoolbc.ca, so mention Langford when contacting us.

Do you offer pickup in Colwood, Westhills and Sooke?

Yes. Our Westshore pickup areas include Colwood, Westhills, View Royal, Metchosin and Sooke, as well as Langford, Goldstream Avenue and Millstream Village. Give us your address and preferred time so we can confirm the pickup point, schedule and any travel charge.

Will I practise the exact ICBC test route around Langford or Colwood?

We can help with local driving and road-test preparation, but we do not promise an official or exact test route. ICBC does not make its routes available outside ICBC. Tell us the office shown on your appointment so we can plan relevant preparation without treating a practice route as your exam route.

ICBC road-test booking and route information

Can I book Class 7 or Class 5 preparation at the Langford Campus?

Yes. We offer Class 7 learner and novice support and Class 5 road-test preparation where that test is required. Confirm your individual licensing requirements with ICBC, then call 250-542-3673 or email us with your licence stage, goals and test date if booked.
