Frequently Asked Questions

How can I prepare for the B.C. knowledge test?

Start with the current ICBC guide for your licence class. Use practice questions to review road signs, traffic rules, safe-driving decisions, and other relevant topics, then return to the guide whenever you miss or are unsure about a rule.

How many questions are on the knowledge test in B.C.?

The format depends on the licence class and testing method. Check ICBC's current information for your specific test before your appointment. This independent tool uses up to 20 questions and does not reproduce the official test format.

What is the Class 7 knowledge test?

It is the passenger-vehicle knowledge test used when working toward a B.C. learner's licence. It assesses road signs, traffic rules, safe-driving practices, and related driving knowledge.

What is the Class 5 knowledge test?

ICBC currently groups Class 5 and 7 together as its passenger-vehicle knowledge test. The requirements that apply to you can depend on your licensing situation, so confirm your required test and next steps directly with ICBC.

What is the Class 4 knowledge test?

A Class 4 knowledge test covers requirements for the applicable commercial passenger licence. This tool does not contain a separate Class 4 question bank; use ICBC's current commercial driving guide and licensing information.

What is the Class 6 knowledge test?

Class 6 is a motorcycle licence class. Motorcycle testing includes motorcycle-specific knowledge that is outside this passenger-vehicle practice bank, so use ICBC's current motorcycle guide and official resources.

What is the Class 1 knowledge test?

Class 1 is a commercial licence class with commercial-driver knowledge requirements. This tool does not provide Class 1-specific practice. Review ICBC's current commercial licensing and study materials instead.

Can I take ICBC knowledge test practice online?

Yes. You can use this independent tool from home and ICBC also provides an official online practice test. This site's questions are not supplied, reviewed, approved, or endorsed by ICBC.

What are knowledge test practice questions?

They are sample questions designed to help you review topics that may appear in B.C. driving knowledge testing, such as road signs, traffic rules, road markings, hazard awareness, and safe-driving decisions.

Are these practice questions the same as the official ICBC test?

No. They are independent sample questions for study and are not copies of live ICBC test questions. Use them to check your understanding, then verify the rules with ICBC's official guide and practice test.

How often should I use knowledge test practice questions?

Use practice regularly enough to identify weak topics, review the related sections of the official guide, and try a fresh set. Understanding the rule behind an answer is more useful than memorising an answer choice.

Understand the Rule Behind Each Answer

At Shanaya's Driving School, we recommend using practice questions alongside official ICBC study materials. The goal is not simply to remember whether an answer is A, B, C, or D. It is to understand the driving rule behind the answer so you can apply it when the wording or situation changes.

This question bank is intended for passenger-vehicle study. If you are preparing for a motorcycle, commercial, or another licence class, use practice resources and official guides written for that specific test.
