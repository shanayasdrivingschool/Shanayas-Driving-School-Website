Victoria Transit Strike Planned for November 27: What Is Confirmed

BC Transit confirms a November 27, 2026 bus suspension. Read the service exceptions, picket locations and travel checklist for Victoria and the Westshore.

By Shanaya's Driving School · Published November 24, 2026 · 4 min read · Greater Victoria Travel Updates

BC Transit and Unifor have announced a one-day Victoria transit strike for Friday, November 27, 2026. The employer's service notice confirms a planned fixed-route bus suspension, with exceptions for Victoria handyDART and interregional routes 66 and 99. For Victoria, Langford and Westshore riders, the immediate task is to check every connection and arrange a return trip.

News check: November 24, 2026. The announced action is scheduled for November 27. Actual service arrangements may change while bargaining continues.

A trip on route 66 or 99 may still leave you without a local connection. BC Transit's earlier rolling-strike advisory specifically warns that local conventional service will not be available beyond designated drop-off points during a regional suspension. Confirm how you will complete that final leg.

Where are pickets planned?

The November 20 notice lists the Victoria Transit Centre, BC Transit's head office at 520 Gorge Road East, and the Langford Transit Centre at 1000 Henry Eng Place.

BC Transit's office-access guidance says the head office closes to the public while pickets are present. Taxi Saver vouchers and handyDART fare products normally sold there will be temporarily unavailable; online store purchases may also be affected. The office-sales interruption is separate from handyDART's status in the dispute.

What led to the November 27 announcement?

Unifor's November 20 statement says Local 333-BC represents approximately 790 operators and mechanics, and their collective agreement expired on March 31, 2025. The union says it proposed mediation and binding arbitration, and that BC Transit declined arbitration. These bargaining claims are attributed to Unifor, rather than presented as an independent finding by the school.

BC Transit's response says it remains committed to negotiations and hopes to reach a fair, sustainable agreement. Its service notice does not provide a detailed response to the union's arbitration claim.

What should Westshore riders arrange now?

For a Langford-to-Victoria commute, a class or an appointment, plan the complete door-to-door journey. A ride into town is only half a plan if your return depends on an unavailable connection. Use this checklist with everyone involved in the trip:

  1. Write down the journey. Record the destination, required arrival time, bus routes and transfers. Identify which part of the journey needs an alternative.
  2. Confirm the alternative directly. Ask a household member or friend about a ride, or check a taxi or ride-hailing option with its provider. Confirm the booking and fare; this article does not establish availability.
  3. Ask about flexibility early. Contact your employer, school or appointment provider about a different time or remote option. An announced transit disruption does not itself confirm a change to their arrangements.
  4. Include the return trip. Agree on a pickup location, time, contact number and backup if the first arrangement falls through.
  5. Recheck before travelling. Open the official alert page the evening before and again before departure. Check the date of any screenshot or forwarded message.

Keep the plan in one message that everyone helping with transport can read. Include the person to contact if you will be late. This avoids arranging a lift while leaving the meeting point or return time unresolved.

If you have a driving lesson or ICBC appointment

Tell your instructor if you normally take a bus to your lesson meeting point. Confirm the meeting location and time before changing your travel arrangements. If your appointment is with ICBC, use your booking confirmation to contact ICBC about any change you need. The transit notice does not confirm cancellation of an individual road test.
