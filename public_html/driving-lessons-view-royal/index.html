How your View Royal lessons work

  1. Discuss experience and goals

    Share what you have practised already and what feels difficult. Agree on an appropriate starting point.

  2. Work on local-road skills

    Focus on observation, turns, lane changes or another agreed task, with feedback as the lesson progresses.

  3. Plan highway preparation

    When appropriate, discuss a separate progression to on-ramps and highway driving. Feeling confident alone does not determine readiness.

  4. Review preparation needs

    Discuss remaining practice and, if useful, a Road Test Prep lesson or Mock Test Evaluation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I work on Highway 1 merging?

Highway preparation can be a lesson goal. Tell us when booking, and let the instructor assess the appropriate stage, location and conditions for that practice.

Can I request pickup near Victoria General Hospital?

Send the exact address and preferred time to the team. We will check whether a suitable meeting point and lesson are available; pickup is not confirmed until the arrangements are agreed.

Where do View Royal lessons start?

Confirm your meeting point with the team. Our office is at 124-2770 Leigh Rd, Langford, BC V9B 4G1, and another starting location may be arranged subject to availability.

Can I start if busy roads make me nervous?

Tell us before the lesson so we can discuss pacing and a manageable starting point. Your instructor will consider both your comfort and demonstrated driving skills when planning progression.

Can I book road-test preparation?

Ask about a Road Test Prep lesson or Mock Test Evaluation when booking. These are separate course options; a mock test is a practice assessment, not an ICBC test or a guarantee of passing.

Can I use a school vehicle for my road test?

Ask about the Road Test Package with your test date and location. Vehicle availability, price and inclusions need separate confirmation.
