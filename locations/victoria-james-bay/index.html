Questions about James Bay and Victoria lessons

Where is your Victoria Campus?

Our Victoria Campus (James Bay) is at 503 Niagara St, Victoria, BC V8V 2G4, Canada. This is our James Bay branch in British Columbia, separate from our Langford Campus on Leigh Road. Mention Victoria or James Bay when using our shared phone number or email.

Can you pick me up in James Bay or near Beacon Hill Park?

Yes. Door-to-door pickup is available in James Bay and the Beacon Hill Park area, with additional coverage in Downtown Victoria, Fairfield, Oak Bay, Fernwood, Esquimalt and Saanich. Give us your exact address and preferred time to confirm a suitable pickup point and any travel charge.

Can I prepare with you for an ICBC test at Victoria/McKenzie Avenue?

Yes. Tell us that your confirmed appointment is at the Victoria/McKenzie office and share the test class and date. We can discuss a preparation plan from James Bay. Your school lesson and ICBC appointment are separate bookings; confirm the test location and arrival instructions with ICBC.

Find your ICBC driver licensing office

Will lessons cover parallel parking, downtown traffic and the McKenzie test route?

Parallel parking, city traffic and road-test preparation can be lesson goals. We select practice according to your skills and current conditions. ICBC does not share official routes outside ICBC, so we prepare driving skills rather than promise the route you will take on test day.
